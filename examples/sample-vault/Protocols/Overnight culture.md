# Overnight culture

## Purpose
Grow a 5 mL overnight *E. coli* culture from a glycerol stock, ready for a miniprep the next day.

## Safety
- PPE: lab coat and gloves.
- Hazards: work next to a flame or in the hood; autoclave used tips and tubes.

## Materials
| Reagent or equipment | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LB medium | 5 mL | |
| Ampicillin, 100 mg/mL | 5 µL | final 100 µg/mL |
| Glycerol stock | a scraping | keep on dry ice |
| 14 mL culture tube | 1 | |
| Shaking incubator | | 37 °C, 220 rpm |

## Steps
1. [ ] Add 5 mL LB and 5 µL ampicillin to a culture tube.
2. [ ] Scrape the frozen glycerol stock with a sterile tip and drop the tip into the tube. Don't let the stock thaw.
3. [ ] Label the tube with the strain, date and your initials.
4. [ ] Grow 12–16 h at 37 °C, shaking at 220 rpm.
5. [ ] Check the culture is turbid before using it.

## Expected results
A turbid culture, OD600 above 2.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No growth | Wrong antibiotic, or the stock thawed | Check the resistance marker; streak a plate first |
