# Plasmid miniprep

## Purpose
Isolate plasmid DNA from a 2 mL overnight *E. coli* culture using a silica spin column.

## Safety
- PPE: lab coat, gloves, safety glasses.
- Hazards: P2 lysis buffer contains NaOH; N3 neutralisation buffer contains guanidine hydrochloride (irritant). Dispose of column flow-through as chemical waste.

## Materials
| Reagent or equipment | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overnight culture in LB + antibiotic | 2 mL | OD600 above 2 |
| P1 resuspension buffer | 250 µL | with RNase A, kept at 4 °C |
| P2 lysis buffer | 250 µL | |
| N3 neutralisation buffer | 350 µL | |
| PE wash buffer | 750 µL | with ethanol added |
| Elution buffer (10 mM Tris, pH 8.5) | 50 µL | |
| Spin column and 2 mL collection tube | 1 | |
| Microcentrifuge | | 13,000 × g |

## Steps
1. [ ] Pellet 2 mL of culture, 1 min at 13,000 × g. Discard the supernatant.
2. [ ] Resuspend the pellet in 250 µL P1 until no clumps remain.
3. [ ] Add 250 µL P2 and invert 4–6 times. Do not vortex. Lyse for **no more than 5 min**.
4. [ ] Add 350 µL N3 and invert immediately 4–6 times. The solution turns cloudy.
5. [ ] Spin 10 min at 13,000 × g.
6. [ ] Load the supernatant onto the column. Spin 1 min and discard the flow-through.
7. [ ] Wash with 750 µL PE. Spin 1 min and discard the flow-through.
8. [ ] Spin 1 min more to dry the membrane.
9. [ ] Place the column in a clean 1.5 mL tube, add 50 µL elution buffer to the centre of the membrane, wait 1 min, spin 1 min.
10. [ ] Measure concentration and A260/A280. Label the tube and register it as a sample.

## Expected results
50–150 ng/µL from a high-copy plasmid; A260/A280 between 1.8 and 1.9.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low yield | Culture overgrown or low-copy plasmid | Use a fresh 12–16 h culture; elute in 30 µL |
| Genomic DNA contamination | Vortexed after P2, or lysed too long | Invert gently; keep lysis under 5 min |
| Low A260/A280 | Ethanol or salt carry-over | Repeat the dry spin; make sure PE has ethanol |
